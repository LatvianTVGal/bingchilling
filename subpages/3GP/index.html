3GP - the backbone of the internet (in the 2010s)

If you have been alive back in the late 2000s - early 2010s, you might have
used or seen 3gp in action.


Blocky, compressed video in 15fps, audio like it came from
RealPlayer 5 on 56k dial-up, all the jazz. There is even one available on this very website - click here!


The age of the 3rd generation of mobile connectivity (also known as 3G), brought in high (at that time) speeds, which
were crucial in making early video streaming possible for the masses
(before that - the only streaming which would be possible would be done via RealPlayer and 2G, which was and is less than ideal).

This page is an adage of sorts to it, in the future you will be able to upload your own 3gps here.
